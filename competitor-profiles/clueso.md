# Clueso
Checked: 2026-11-10. Scope: official MCP and pricing pages.

## Observed
[MCP page](https://www.clueso.io/mcp) describes editable videos from recordings, screenshots and documents, plus brand context, bulk updates and release-video workflows. [Pricing](https://www.clueso.io/pricing) lists Free, Solo at $40/month billed yearly, and Business at $120/month billed yearly for two users. Annual export allowances shown are three hours for Solo and six for that Business configuration. A promotional banner is separate from these listed rates.

## Interpretation
A substantial competitor for teams producing libraries of customer education and training material. Generic bulk updates, brand memory and release-video integrations are already occupied territory.

## Implication for Glint
Tie refreshes to a real, repeatable application workflow and evidence that the demonstrated outcome still happens. Avoid competing first on a broad enterprise content suite.

## Uncertainty
Checkout, promotion terms and agent reliability were not tested. No claim that Clueso lacks selective refresh; that workflow was not established in this review.
