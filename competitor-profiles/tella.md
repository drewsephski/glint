# Tella
Checked: 2026-11-10. Scope: official MCP, Auto Capture and pricing pages.

## Observed
[MCP](https://www.tella.com/mcp) supports editing, exports and publishing. [Auto Capture](https://www.tella.com/auto-capture), advertised in beta, records web walkthroughs remotely; its examples already include PR demos and help articles. It explicitly excludes localhost, intranet hosts and desktop/mobile apps. [Pricing](https://www.tella.com/pricing) lists annual-billing rates of $13/user/month Pro and $19 Premium, with monthly AI credits.

## Interpretation
The strongest challenge to a generic “tell an agent to make a demo” pitch. Capture automation and release workflows are already marketed. Local/private targets offer a narrower distinction, though other competitors also operate locally.

## Implication for Glint
Test local walkthrough maintenance and outcome evidence against Tella's automated first-draft workflow. Do not present PR-to-video as unique.

## Uncertainty
Beta access and capture reliability were not tested. Some older vendor pages show different credit allowances; the current pricing page is the reference.
